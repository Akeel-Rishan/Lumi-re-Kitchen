# Lumière Kitchen: brand direction

A fictional Manchester restaurant for the Arizonix portfolio. Locale en-GB, currency GBP and timezone Europe/London stay in presentation configuration. The identity is warm, considered and welcoming: editorial typography, generous breathing room and a clear hierarchy, with minimal ornament.

## One family, two settings

Public pages use warm ivory, dark ink, evergreen emphasis and Georgia editorial headings. The existing temporary welcome screen uses a restrained typographic identity, a short restaurant introduction and an honest reservation-development notice. It is not the final homepage.

Admin uses light neutral backgrounds, white working surfaces, sans-serif headings, compact spacing and tabular numerals. Strong borders distinguish essential controls; text labels distinguish statuses. Long operational sessions take priority over decoration. No admin route or shell exists yet.

Bronze is a decorative rule/detail, never small text or the sole essential control boundary. Evergreen supports links, actions and focus. Neutral borders separate content without making every section a card. Use only the approved combinations in [design tokens](design-tokens.md).

## Type and imagery

Georgia's established serif forms provide an editorial voice; Segoe UI with Arial/Helvetica fallbacks supplies readable interface text. Intentional system stacks avoid font downloads, licensing uncertainty and build-time network dependencies. Rendering varies across operating systems; see [typography and layout](typography-and-layout.md).

Later photography should show natural light, true food colour, human scale and candid hospitality. Use properly licensed, accurately labelled imagery. No photography, fabricated logo, placeholder asset or image dependency is necessary now.

Appropriate: one expressive heading, readable paragraphs, evergreen recovery actions, a small bronze rule, whitespace with purpose. Inappropriate: glass panels, neon, decorative animation, gradients, low-contrast gold text, enormous mobile type, repeated oversized cards or operational-dashboard decoration on public pages.

## Honest demonstration content

Keep the Arizonix disclosure and fictional-restaurant notice visible. Do not invent contact details, menus, awards, reviews, booking availability or business results. Sample operational records must later be labelled demonstration data. A pending request must never look like confirmation. Copy follows the [lifecycle contract](../domain/reservation-lifecycle.md); visual treatment cannot change behaviour.
